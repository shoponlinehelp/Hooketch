our story

Hi, I'm Iqra 👋

I started Hooketch because I love making small things with yarn and a hook. What began as a hobby is now my small business, and I am building it step by step.

Every keychain, flower, bow and bag you see here is crocheted by me, stitch by stitch. No machines. Just time, care and a lot of love.

Want a different colour, size or a special design? Just write to me. I love making custom pieces. If you want something new for your own shop or as a gift, email me and we will make it together. 💕
